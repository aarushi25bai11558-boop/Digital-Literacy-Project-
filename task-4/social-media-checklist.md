The "Do" Checklist
[ ] Curate Your Privacy Settings: Regularly review who can see your posts. Use "Close Friends" lists or private profiles for personal content.

[ ] Network Professionally: Use platforms like LinkedIn to follow companies in your field, connect with alumni, and share your academic milestones.

[ ] Think Before You Post: Ask yourself if you’d be comfortable with a future employer, professor, or your parents seeing the content.

[ ] Verify Information: Before sharing a news story or "viral" fact, check its credibility to avoid spreading misinformation.

[ ] Give Credit: If you are sharing someone else's art, research, or photography, always tag the original creator.



The "Don't" Checklist
[ ] Don't Post During Class: Avoid the temptation to live-post or scroll during lectures; it’s disrespectful to the instructor and distracting to peers.

[ ] Don't Over-Share Personal Details: Avoid posting your specific location in real-time, your student ID number, or your home address.

[ ] Don't Engage with Trolls: Avoid "keyboard wars." Emotional or aggressive arguments can be screenshotted and taken out of context later.

[ ] Don't Badmouth Your University: Complaining about specific professors or staff online can lead to disciplinary issues or bridge-burning.

[ ] Don't Ignore Your Digital Footprint: Never assume that "deleting" a post makes it gone forever. Once it's online, it can be saved by anyone.
